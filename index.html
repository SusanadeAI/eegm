OUR HERITAGE

The Birth of a Global Movement

A testimony of unwavering prayer, consecration, and God's faithfulness.

From Humble Beginnings

Eternity Echoes Global Ministries was established in the year 2021 with just 4 members and a marathon fast, tagged "MEN OF ENCOUNTERS". We began holding vigils every Tuesday (with the last Tuesday acting as an interdenominational edition).

Our journey began at the Ondo State National Library, then moved to the Holy Ghost Church at Oluwatuyi Akure, and later to Re360 Agent Development at Sijuwade Akure. Today, we stand as a registered entity (CAC 2023), continuing our mission from our current venue at GroundFloor, Diamond Hotel, Alagbaka GRA, Akure, Ondo state.

Setman: Evangelist Elisha Olorunda & Minstrel Opeyemi Elisha Olorunda.
