Founder
About The Founder

Evangelist Elisha Olorunda

Evangelist Elisha Olorunda is a passionate preacher of the Gospel of Jesus Christ and the founder of Eternity Echoes Global Ministries. He is widely known for proclaiming the UNDILUTED GOOD NEWS of Jesus Christ, with a strong emphasis on salvation, healing, and the transforming power of God's grace.

Through his crusade outreaches and evangelistic missions, many souls have been saved, and numerous testimonies of instant miracles and healings have been recorded.

"His ministry focuses on reaching people where they are and presenting the message of Jesus in a clear, practical, and powerful way that reveals the love and finished work of Christ."

He is recognized as a major pioneer of the JESUS CRUSADE movement, where the Gospel is taken directly to communities and nations. These crusades are marked by powerful demonstrations of God's presence through healings, deliverance, and salvation, leading many people into a life-changing encounter with Jesus Christ.

Evangelist Elisha also leads Elisha Olorunda Ministries, a broadcast ministry committed to building, encouraging, and inspiring people through the Gospel. Through teachings, outreach programs, and online content, the ministry continues to reach audiences both locally and internationally.

His ministry passion is centered on soul-winning, healing evangelism, and the global proclamation of the Gospel.

Today, Evangelist Elisha Olorunda continues to raise believers who boldly proclaim the message of Christ and carry the Gospel to every corner of the world, accompanied by signs and wonders.

Our Heritage

The Birth of a Global Movement.

Eternity Echoes Global Ministries was established in the year 2021 with just 4 members and a marathon fast, tagged "MEN OF ENCOUNTERS". We began holding vigils every Tuesday (with the last Tuesday acting as an interdenominational edition).

Our journey began at the Ondo State National Library, then moved to the Holy Ghost Church at Oluwatuyi Akure, and later to Re360 Agent Development at Sijuwade Akure. Today, we stand as a registered entity (CAC 2023), continuing our mission from our current venue at Nepa Opposite Immigration Office.

The Setman of Eternity Echoes Global Ministries is the person of Evangelist Elisha Olorunda and his amazing wife, Minstrel Opeyemi Elisha Olorunda.
